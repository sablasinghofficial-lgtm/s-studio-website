The Sabla Singh Difference

Six foundational pillars that distinguish our atelier and academy across Jharkhand and Eastern India.

20 Years of Proven Mastery

Two decades of deep cosmetic refinement, continuous international training updates, and enduring generational trust among royal families of Ranchi.

Personalized Beauty Curation

Never one-size-fits-all. Every lip pigment, foundation undertone, and hair ornament is selected to accentuate individual facial architecture and skin health.

Global Cosmetic Standards

Exclusively authentic premium brands: MAC Pro, Charlotte Tilbury, Tom Ford, Bobbi Brown, Kryolan Professional, and Temptu Airbrush formulations.

1-on-1 Practical Mentorship

Our academy strictly caps class sizes. Each learner receives real-time posture corrections, stroke guidance, and direct feedback from Sabla Singh herself.

Meticulous Detail & Punctuality

We honor the sanctity of wedding timelines. Pre-scheduled milestones ensure you step into your ceremony composed, radiant, and without rush.

Private, Ultra-Hygienic Atelier

Medical-grade UV brush sterilization, single-use disposables, and private luxury vanity lounges to ensure serenity and complete safety.
